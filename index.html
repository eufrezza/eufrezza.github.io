About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Economics at the Università della Svizzera italiana and a Research Fellow at the Women and Public Policy Program at the Harvard Kennedy School.

My research lies at the intersection of development economics, gender, and political economy. I study how norms, beliefs, and behaviour interact, with a particular emphasis on gender-based violence.

I received my PhD in Economics from Trinity College Dublin in 2024. My CV can be found here.
